Feb 2026 — Present Active Appointment

Xu Lab, Carnegie Mellon University

Research Intern (Remote) · 3D Brain MRI Surface Mesh Registration

Developing deep learning pipelines that perform non-rigid anatomical registration between predicted cortical surface meshes and ground-truth MRI representations. Focuses on preserving geometric topology, surface smoothness, and anatomical landmark fidelity across diverse clinical populations.

  • Formulated an end-to-end deep registration framework designed to maintain spatial topology without collapsing complex cortical sulcal and gyral folds.
  • Engineered custom pipeline scripts interfacing FreeSurfer's volumetric reconstruction (recon-all) with 3D mesh rendering tools in Freeview, inspecting raw .mgz and .obj surface representations directly.
  • Published an end-to-end preprocessed IXI benchmark subject run through FreeSurfer's multi-hour pipeline to lower the prohibitive computational barrier for fellow 3D neuroimaging researchers.
Research Pipeline Architecture: T1 MRI Scans ➔ Volumetric Reconstruction (FreeSurfer recon-all) ➔ Spherical Mapping ➔ Cortical Surface Extraction (.mgz/.obj) ➔ Deep Surface Registration Network ➔ Topological Evaluation in Freeview

Oct 2025 — Present Academic Research

GTU — MedOptm Clinical VLM

Undergraduate Researcher with Prof. Raj Hakani · Ahmedabad

Engineered a lightweight, high-accuracy vision-language model (VLM) for clinical visual question answering, overcoming the prohibitive parameter scale and memory requirements of traditional foundation models.

  • Integrated a Convolutional Vision Transformer (CvT-24) encoder with an architectural Token Packer to dramatically compress visual tokens before projection into a 1.7B-parameter LLaMA language backbone.
  • Constructed an automated high-throughput data curation and biomedical captioning pipeline, enabling end-to-end model fine-tuning on consumer hardware without large cluster access.
  • Achieved a 0.33 METEOR score on clinical question answering benchmarks while maintaining a sub-2B parameter footprint for edge clinical usability.
MedOptm Model Architecture: Input Scan ➔ CvT-24 (Conv Projections) ➔ Token Packer (Spatial Compression: 256 ➔ 64 tokens) ➔ Linear Projection ➔ LLaMA 1.7B LLM ➔ Clinical Diagnosis & Explanations
METEOR 0.33 · Sub-2B Parameter Footprint

Jun 2025 — Aug 2025 Published Research

GTU — Two-Stage Brain Tumor MRI Classification

Lead Undergraduate Researcher with Prof. Raj Hakani · Ahmedabad

Formulated a deep transfer learning classification framework designed for accurate multi-class brain MRI scan diagnosis across heterogeneous clinical scanner datasets.

  • Devised a two-stage training strategy: initial training of specialized classification heads on a frozen VGG16 backbone, followed by targeted unfreezing and end-to-end fine-tuning.
  • Reported 97.88% classification accuracy across glioma, meningioma, pituitary, and no-tumor scan classes. Accepted for presentation and publication at ICMLDE 2025.
97.88% Accuracy · Accepted at ICMLDE 2025
