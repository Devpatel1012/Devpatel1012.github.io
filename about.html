ICMLDE 2025 · Peer-Reviewed Conference Paper Accepted for Publication

Two-Stage Transfer Learning with Modified VGG16 for Multi-Class Brain Tumor Classification on Heterogeneous MRI

Dev Delvadiya (Dev Patel), Raj Hakani

Presents a two-stage training protocol designed to maximize generalization across multi-scanner brain MRI datasets. Demonstrates that training a specialized classification head on frozen features followed by end-to-end backbone fine-tuning reliably avoids catastrophic representation collapse while reaching 97.88% test accuracy.
