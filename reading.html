03 // READING SHELF & FOUNDATIONAL LITERATURE

The shelf.

A curated log of foundational books directly informing my research methodology — each annotated with a concrete explanation of how it shaped my model designs and mathematical intuitions.

Curated Foundational Literature

01
Dimensionality Reduction in Machine Learning Jamal Amani Rad Why it mattered for my work: Provides geometric intuition for manifold regularization, which directly guides how I constrain latent token projections in compact multimodal encoders.
Active Reading
02
Mathematics for Machine Learning Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal & Cheng Soon Ong Why it mattered for my work: Grounds the vector calculus, orthogonal projection, and SVD mechanics necessary when calculating non-rigid 3D mesh deformation gradients.
Completed
03
Deep Learning with PyTorch Eli Stevens, Luca Antiga & Thomas Viehmann Why it mattered for my work: Taught me low-level tensor stride mechanics, custom autograd function authoring, and memory profiling needed for CUDA-efficient attention layers.
Completed
04
Hands-On Large Language Models Jay Alammar & Maarten Grootendorst Why it mattered for my work: Clarified token projection and cross-attention bottleneck behavior, informing the Token Packer design in the MedOptm architecture.
Completed
05
Calculus for Machine Learning Stefania Cristina & Mehreen Saeed Why it mattered for my work: Solidified Jacobian and Hessian formulation through multi-stage computational graphs, critical for two-stage transfer learning stability.
Completed
06
Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) Survey & Foundations Anthology Why it mattered for my work: Informs my current reading on reward model calibration and preference optimization techniques for clinical reasoning alignment.
Active Reading
